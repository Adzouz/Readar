Readar

Pick the topics you care about. Readar listens to blogs, news sites, YouTube and podcasts for you, writes a short summary of everything new, and lets you swipe through it in a few minutes a day.

  • WatchTopics and sources, checked every hour
  • SummarizeJudge any item in ten seconds, in your language
  • Swipe← dismiss · → like · ↑ read later
  • SyncMac, iPhone and web from day one

How it works

The loop

Every swipe feeds back into what Readar shows you next.

  1. 1Pick topicsTap big topic tiles or type any keyword. Each topic gets its own lane.
  2. 2Readar listensEvery hour it checks your sources: site feeds, YouTube channels, podcasts, web pages and keyword searches.
  3. 3AI sortsMerges duplicate stories, scores relevance and writes a two-line summary.
  4. 4You swipe← dismiss, → like, ↑ read later. Undo is always one key away.
  5. 5Lists follow youLiked, Read later and Dismissed sync to every device you use.

AI

Summaries and ranking

Claude Sonnet 5.5 writes each summary only when someone is about to see it. In the MVP every user connects their own Claude API key: summaries are written on their device and stay private to their account.

What a card says

TL;DROne sentence that answers "should I care?"
Key pointsThree bullets, shown with Space.
Why youWhich interests it matches, or "like 2 posts you liked".
Time"7 min read → 30 s summary"
CoverageOne card per story, "also covered by 4 sources".
LanguageWritten in your language, even when the source isn't.

How ranking learns

  • Topic matchEach topic has a meaning vector built from its name, keywords and examples. Items score by similarity.
  • Your taste→ pulls the topic toward an item, ← pushes it away. After a week the deck feels personal.
  • Source trustSources you often like rank higher; ones you always dismiss fade, and Readar offers to mute them.
  • FreshnessNewer wins when relevance is equal.

Shown on each card as signal strength: one to three bars.

Plans

Plans, after the MVP

The MVP has no plans: everyone connects their own Claude API key. This lineup arrives with the public launch. Fetching and sync cost little; summaries are the real cost.

Free

Try the loop

  • A few topics
  • A daily quota of AI summaries
  • Feeds, YouTube and keyword watches
  • Share pages
Pro

Readar pays for AI

  • Unlimited topics and summaries, within fair use
  • Podcasts and sites without a feed
  • Hourly refresh
  • Priced to cover Sonnet 5.5 for a heavy reader
Pro + own key

Your Claude, cheaper plan

  • Everything in Pro
  • Summaries billed to your own Claude API key
  • Readar charges only for fetching, sync and storage

How "own key" works

  • Create an API key in Claude Console and paste it in Settings. Readar checks it with one small test call.
  • MVP: the key stays in your device's keychain (browser storage on web) and never reaches Readar's servers. Summaries are written on your device and synced to your other devices.
  • Summaries written with your key stay private to your account. After launch, summaries already in Readar's shared library will cost you nothing.
  • Settings suggests giving the key its own spending limit in Console.
  • If the key fails or runs out of credit, summaries pause and cards show the source's own excerpt until it's fixed.

Why not "Sign in with Claude"?

Anthropic doesn't let third-party apps offer Claude.ai login or use Free, Pro, Max or Ultra plan credentials for their users. A Claude Console API key, billed to its owner, is the allowed path.

Roadmap

MVP first, then the public launch

Phases 0 to 2 are the MVP on Mac, iPhone and web. Phase 3 adds plans, share pages, more languages and the store launch.

Phase 0

Foundations and spikes

  • Monorepo: Tauri 2 app for Mac and iPhone, web build, worker, shared packages
  • Theme tokens for Native Glass, Editorial Calm and Playful Deck, light and dark
  • Spikes on iPhone: sign-in via deep link, share sheet, Safari view, swipe performance
  • Summary prompt checked on about 50 real articles with Sonnet 5.5
  • Claude key in the device keychain; first real summaries on device
  • CI for lint, types and tests
Phase 1

Core loop on Mac and web

  • Onboarding: style, topics, sources, Claude key; email sign-in after the first deck
  • Topic catalog with curated sources; add your own by URL, channel, keyword or OPML
  • Worker: feeds, YouTube, keyword watches, Hacker News, dedupe
  • Deck per topic with ← → ↑, Space and undo; summaries written on device; ranking
  • Liked, Read later, Dismissed, synced across devices
Phase 2

iPhone and the rest of v1

  • iPhone app: gestures, haptics, share sheet, Safari view
  • Page watcher for sites without a feed; podcasts with transcripts
  • Share the article link from any card
Phase 3

Public launch

  • Free, Pro and Pro + own key, through the App Store and Stripe
  • Share pages on readar.news; French, Spanish, German, Portuguese (Brazil)
  • Sign in with Apple, in-app account deletion, data export
  • Terms, privacy policy and publisher policy
  • Mac App Store and App Store review; listings in five languages
  • Landing page on getreadar.com; analytics and crash reporting

After v1: Android, a newsletter inbox, Bluesky and Mastodon, a morning briefing notification, a Mac menu-bar companion, widgets.
